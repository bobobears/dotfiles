# 觅长生 Confabulation Case Study

## What happened

User asked: "觅长生竹山宗炼丹，选择哪个品种比较好" (which herb varietal to plant for alchemy in the Bamboo Mountain sect of the game "Seeking Immortality").

**Wrong answer given:** I listed "蛇涎草、腐骨花、紫芝" (snake-slobber grass, bone-rotting flower, purple mushroom) as poison-attribute herbs for poison-type alchemy.

**User correction:** "部分内容，包括所谓毒系丹药，游戏中没有" — these items and poison-type alchemy do not exist in the game.

## Root cause

- The game has a poison-themed sect (竹山宗 uses poison-based combat skills)
- I extrapolated "poison sect → must have poison herbs and poison alchemy"
- The actual game's alchemy system simply doesn't have "poison-type" as a separate category
- I generated plausible-sounding Chinese fantasy herb names that fit the genre but aren't in the game

## Lessons

1. A sect's **combat theme** ≠ its **crafting system**. Combat and crafting are separate game systems that may draw from different design spaces.
2. Never extrapolate specific item names from a general thematic direction.
3. When you can't find the answer on a wiki/guide, it's better to say "I couldn't find specific information" than to fill gaps with what sounds right.
